# Atan Burhagohain — Pilot production pack

Indian Heroes • v0.1 • 1 November 2026

## Production brief

Working title: **Atan — Taaj Se Bada Faisla**.

Stage: research and preproduction. This pack contains a six-episode treatment, a first-Reel narration draft, proposed visual continuity, and a timed creative shotlist. Subsequent episode scripts, full-film narration, reference images, voice, generated clips and exports are not yet produced.

Format: six connected Reels, approximately 45–60 seconds each, composed for 9:16; a separately edited continuous 4–6-minute 16:9 YouTube film. These are creative targets, not measured audio or provider clip durations. Start with Episode 1 as the pilot before scaling.

Audience: Hindi/Hinglish-speaking viewers who need no prior knowledge of Ahom history. Use clear conversational narration, photorealistic cinematic historical drama and restrained drama. Keep violence non-graphic. This is a historical biography; supernatural abilities would require a separate fictional adaptation.

Creative premise: a leader must defend a threatened kingdom, then navigate the dangerous politics within it. His defining screen trait is attention: he listens, weighs consequences and acts deliberately. Show his choices through people and physical tasks instead of lengthy exposition.

## Episode 1 — spoken Hinglish draft

Target: approximately 55–60 seconds, including pauses. Read aloud before setting final shot boundaries. Roman-script spelling below is a narration aid, not a verified Assamese phonetic transcription.

> Raja banne ka mauka mile, toh kaun mana karega?
>
> Assam ke Atan Burhagohain ne kiya. Do baar.
>
> Lekin pehle kahaani ko peechhe le chalte hain.
>
> Saal 1662. Mughal senapati Mir Jumla Assam ki rajdhani Garhgaon tak pahunch gaya. Raja ko peechhe hatna pada.
>
> Isi saal Atan bane Burhagohain—Ahom rajya ke ek bade mantri.
>
> Unhone Mughal sena ke khilaaf resistance sangathit kiya. Aage chal kar rajya ko dobara sambhalne mein bhi unki aham bhoomika rahi.
>
> Yahin se hamaari kahaani shuru hoti hai. Ek aise aadmi ki, jise aage Lachit Borphukan ke saath bhi ladna tha.
>
> Par agla sawaal tha: is hamle ke baad Assam dobara khada kaise hoga?

Claim mapping: lines 1–2 → C07; invasion → C02; appointment → C01; resistance/recovery → C03; Lachit setup → C06. Opening crown reference is an explicit flash-forward, followed by a clear 1662 date card. No invented character dialogue.

Narrator direction: curious opening; a short pause after the question and “Do baar”; steady, grounded delivery thereafter. Avoid trailer-style shouting. If the take runs long, simplify wording after listening; do not accelerate the voice unnaturally.

Provider-neutral English prompts, one per shot:

1. **Camera:** slow forward dolly. **Subject:** an empty, minimally ornamented ceremonial seat shown in silhouette. **Look:** the shared photorealistic cinematic style, low warm side light. **Action:** light gradually reveals the unoccupied seat. Symbolic editorial image; do not invent a detailed historical throne.
2. **Camera:** medium three-quarter framing, locked camera. **Subject:** approved Atan court identity and costume references. **Look:** soft daylight, shared style. **Action:** he slowly turns away from the viewer. Quiet restraint. No speech or visible crown-offering ceremony.
3. Build this map in editing from checked geographic data; no image/video generation prompt.
4. **Camera:** static distant wide shot. **Subject:** a marching military column represented as distant silhouettes in a seventeenth-century Assam setting. **Look:** muted haze, shared style. **Action:** the column passes across the shot. Validate weapons and dress against references before submission. No foreground combat or burning buildings.
5. **Camera:** locked medium-wide rear view. **Subject:** anonymous figures in approved period clothing. **Look:** subdued daylight, shared style. **Action:** they walk away along a rural path. Avoid named likenesses, precise royal regalia and unsupported escort details.
6. **Camera:** slight forward push, medium close-up. **Subject:** approved Atan 1662 identity and court costume. **Look:** diffuse daylight, reference-matched interior. **Action:** he listens intently to someone just outside frame. No lip movement or dialogue.
7. **Camera:** eye-level medium framing. **Subject:** approved Atan field reference with two distinct officers in reference-matched clothing. **Look:** grounded shared style, soft overcast light. **Action:** Atan points once towards the same side of frame, officers watching. No sword flourish, map lettering or changing costume.
8. **Camera:** static wide shot. **Subject:** three workers and one timber beam in a reference-checked riverside settlement. **Look:** warm daylight, shared style. **Action:** the workers carry the beam steadily through frame. Respect weight, hand placement and scale. This is an illustrative recovery scene.
9. **Camera:** gentle profile reveal. **Subject:** approved Atan identity, field costume. **Look:** blue-green river light, shared style. **Action:** his gaze settles on the river. Preserve the same hair, face and clothing throughout. No extra named character.
10. **Camera:** slow vertical rise from the riverbank. **Subject:** broad water and distant wooded banks. **Look:** restrained dawn colour, shared style. **Action:** gentle water movement only. Use approved location references. No generated caption or modern bridge.

## Execution and inspection checkpoint

Before provider submission, retrieve current model schemas and costs; choose reference roles, resolution, duration and aspect ratio through actual tool fields. Do not assume a prompt alias binds an image. Record model/schema date, job ID, returned cost, take, actual duration, rejection reason and accepted file path for every submission. No paid generation is authorised by this planning pack.

First production test: E01-S06. It tests the protagonist's identity, costume, interior, expression and restrained movement without the additional failure modes of a battle scene. Inspect the result before scaling to the other shots.

Audio: one narrator identity; native pronunciation review; no character dialogue in pilot. Generate captions from the accepted audio, then manually reconcile names. Sound proposal: subdued water/wood textures and restrained percussion; no invented “authentic Ahom war chant.” Track actual music and SFX rights.

Edit: use editor-built dates, maps and captions. Maintain separate 9:16 and 16:9 timelines, with intentional composition for each. Check caption placement against current platform UI previews. Readability, faces, hands, clothing, river/boat scale and every transition require visual inspection.

Suggested disclosure: “AI-animated historical retelling. Faces and scene staging are artistic reconstructions. Sources are listed in the description.” Add a dramatization notice if invented dialogue is introduced later.

Acceptance boundary: no film is finished until the actual exports are inspected, narration is heard, captions checked and technical metadata verified. This deliverable is a preproduction pilot pack.
